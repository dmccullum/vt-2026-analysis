# Sources: Vermont school consolidation/closure exposure by town

Compiled 2026-11-04. All downloaded files are treated as data only (never executed). Scripts: `xlsx_read.py` (stdlib XLSX reader), `geocode_schools.py`, `build_school_risk.py`. Run with `python3 -I` from this folder.

## Output files
- `school_risk_by_town.csv`: one row per SOS town (247 rows, `tn` list from sos2026/turnOut.json).
- `school_level_vt_2425.csv`: one row per public school (287) with town, grades, 2024-25 enrollment, closure status.
- `school_town_geocode.csv`: AOE F.17 school list plus Census town from lat/long.

## Source 1: School list + enrollment (primary)
1. **AOE Redistricting Task Force data response (official, XLSX)** index: https://map.vermont.gov/education/redistricting-data-requests/ (JSON index: https://vtopendata-prd.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/_Other/Education/data-request.json). Files saved in `aoe_redistricting_data/`:
   - F.17 Public School Locations and Grades (school-level, lat/long, grades, supervisory union, operating district). Used as the school universe (287 active public schools as of ~2025). Note it still lists some schools that closed for SY25-26.
   - F.10 10-Year Enrollment, School Level (2014-2024; sheets K-12 and PK-Adult). Year label "2024" not documented (probably fall 2023 or 2024); included only as `f10_k12_2023/2024` in the school file, not used for thresholds.
   - F.9/F.40 Single-year resident ADM by town, FY20-FY24 (fall 2019 ... fall 2023 census). Used for `resident_adm_fy24`, `resident_adm_fy20`. Sums match statewide totals (excluding unorganized gores).
   - F.47 School Closures Since 2020 (public and independent). Used for closure flag.
   - F.31 facilities (capacity, FCI), F.45 CTE centers by towns, F.17/F.18 district-level data: saved, not used yet.
2. **NCES Common Core of Data 2024-25** (Dec 2025 release): school directory https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/Data/zip/ccd_sch_029_2425_w_1a_073025.zip and membership https://nces.ed.gov/ccd/data/zip/ccd_sch_052_2425_l_1a_073025.zip. VT rows extracted to `nces_dl/vt_directory_2425.csv` and `nces_dl/vt_membership_2425.csv` (national files deleted to save 2.3 GB). `enroll_2024_25_nces` = "Education Unit Total" student count (includes PK). Joined to F.17 on school ID suffix (e.g. PS106). All 287 F.17 schools matched.
3. School to town: CCD/F.17 give only postal city (often a village), so each school's F.17 lat/long was run through the Census TIGERweb county-subdivision layer (https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Places_CouSub_ConCity_SubMCD/MapServer/1). Cross-checked against postal city; one manual fix (PS027 Bellows Free Academy Elementary, Fairfax: F.17 coordinates fell in St. Albans City, set to FAIRFAX). Not verified: Missisquoi Valley UHS (postal Swanton, coordinates fall in Highgate; kept HIGHGATE).
- Limitation: enrollment is a single year (2024-25), not the two-year average Act 73 uses. Union/regional high schools are counted in their host town only. Independent/"town academy" schools (St. Johnsbury Academy, Burr and Burton, Lyndon Institute, etc.) and Rivendell Academy are excluded (not public schools in F.17), so a town can show has_school=0 while hosting one.
- `has_school`=0 does NOT mean no exposure: 55 towns tuition their students out; use `resident_adm_*` for those.

## Source 2: Closure / merger / below-threshold
- **Threshold used**: `n_schools_below_100` = operating K+ schools with 2024-25 enrollment < 100. Rationale: Act 73 defines "small school" as two-year average enrollment under 100 (AOE small school addendum: https://aoa.vermont.gov/sites/aoa/files/RSCA%20Small%20school%20addendum_FINAL.pdf; not fetched directly, cited via search summary). Also `n_schools_below_50`. Act 73 class-size minimums (avg 10 in gr. 1, 12 in gr. 2-5, 15 in gr. 6-8, 18 in gr. 9-12; effective July 2026, summary https://legislature.vermont.gov/Documents/2026/Workgroups/Senate%20Education/Acts%20and%20Previous%20Major%20Legislation/W~Elizabeth%20St.%20James~Act%2073%20of%202025%20High-level%20Overview%20Presentation~1-6-2026.pdf) cannot be computed (no class-level data); a 2026 bill (H.955) reportedly delays some targets (https://www.upforlearning.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/VT-Education-PolicyPanel.pdf). No official list of schools below the thresholds was found.
- **`closure_or_merger_flag`=1** (10 towns): town hosts a school that AOE F.47 lists as closed/PK-only for SY25-26, or whose closure/merger was approved in 2025-26:
  - North Hero, Ripton: F.47 (SY25-26 closed). Greensboro (Lakeview Union) and Essex Junction City (Summit Street): F.47 "now only serving PK" (grade reconfiguration, not necessarily a closure).
  - Marlboro: voters approved closure Mar 3 2026, 311-65 (https://www.wcax.com/2026/03/04/marlboro-residents-vote-close-elementary-school/).
  - Readsboro: approved Mar 3 2026, 222-36 (https://www.wcax.com/2026/03/04/readsboro-votes-close-school/).
  - Sunderland and Danby (Currier Memorial): Taconic and Green board voted 11-2 on Nov 4 2025, closing July 1 2026 (https://vtdigger.org/2025/10/23/a-southern-vermont-school-district-will-weigh-whether-to-close-two-elementary-schools/, https://vtdigger.org/?p=634960). Final Town Meeting outcomes not found; board vote is binding.
  - Jamaica: voters authorized closure Apr 8 2026, 102-36 (https://www.benningtonbanner.com/jamaica-votes-to-close-school-west-river-ed-budget-rejected/article_1afbcc2c-a4e8-4ad0-9dea-87a205f835aa.html); F.47 shows K-12 already ceased SY24-25.
  - Shoreham: K-5 merged into Bridport Central from fall 2026, 8-1 board vote Nov 2025 (https://www.addisonindependent.com/2025/11/26/shoreham-to-send-k-5-students-to-bridport-next-fall/; https://www.addisonindependent.com/2026/06/18/shoreham-bids-farewell-to-its-k-5-school/). Bridport not flagged as a receiving town.
- **`closure_proposed_not_enacted_flag`** (Calais, Worcester, Starksboro, Danville): board voted/proposed but not enacted or unverified. Calais (398-249) and Worcester/Doty (212-114) voters kept schools open Feb 10 2026 (https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-02-11/calais-worcester-vote-keep-elementary-schools-open). Starksboro: Robinson School named as candidate (https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-02-23/town-meeting-day-education-decisions-more-than-school-budgets). Danville HS-grades vote Dec 6 2025: outcome not found. Aug 11 2026 Washington Central vote (1,711-1,365) now lets all five district towns decide small-school closures (https://www.vermontpublic.org/local-news/2026-08-14/washington-central-voters-approve-school-closure-article).
- `n_closed_since_2020_incl_above`: closures counted in the flag plus Roxbury Village (SY24-25), Windham Elementary (SY24-25), Castleton Village (SY22-23) from F.47; those three are not in F.17, towns inferred from school names.
- Ripton: the Addison Central board process is separate from F.47; Readsboro/Marlboro: effective dates "end of 2025-26" per news.
- Limitations: news-based closures are hand-coded and not exhaustive (other 2026 votes may exist); no Vermont Public or VTDigger tracker list was found. Other small schools may face closure votes not recorded here.

## Source 3: Redistricting Task Force proposed districts
- **No town-to-new-district map exists in the final proposal.** The Task Force (final report revised 12/2/2025: https://aoa.vermont.gov/sites/aoa/files/Redistricting%20Task%20Force%20Legislative%20Report_Updated_12.2.25%20v.%202.pdf; Appendix E: https://aoa.vermont.gov/sites/aoa/files/Appendix%20E_%20The%20Vermont%20Regional%20Education%20Partnership%20Model_UPDATED%2012.2.25%20v.%202.pdf) endorsed voluntary mergers with incentives plus five regional cooperative service areas, and no mandated map (VTDigger: https://vtdigger.org/2025/11/11/vermonts-school-redistricting-task-force-proposes-voluntary-mergers-instead-of-new-district-map/). An earlier CTE-region base map (about 14 districts, one Chittenden district of ~22,000 students) was rejected; its PDF was not obtainable. These AOA PDFs return HTTP 403 to scripted downloads; The PDFs were not retrieved. `proposed_district` is therefore blank.
- Interactive tools (not downloadable data): District Builder https://map.vermont.gov/education/ ; School Explorer https://map.vermont.gov/education/school-explorer/.
- F.45 CTE-centers-by-towns XLSX (saved) gives CTE region towns, usable as a proxy for the rejected CTE map; not joined.

## Source 4: Per-town tax change
- **Not obtained by script (see data/raw/tax/ for the browser-retrieved rates).** Vermont Tax Dept publishes town FY2027 and FY2026 education property tax rate tables (XLSX): https://tax.vermont.gov/document/fy2027-education-property-tax-rates and https://tax.vermont.gov/document/fy2026-education-property-tax-rates (page https://tax.vermont.gov/property/education-property-tax-rates). Scripted download returned HTTP 403 (CloudFront); not worked around. These are rate levels under the current formula (statewide ~12% projected rise per Tax Commissioner, Dec 2025), not Act 73 foundation-formula projections, which do not appear to be published by town. `tax_change` is blank. These rates were later retrieved by browser; see data/raw/tax/.
- Also not obtained: AOE Vermont Education Dashboard full enrollment dataset (https://education.vermont.gov/documents/ved-enrollment-dataset returned 403 to scripted fetch); the 2025-26 school-by-school enrollment is therefore not available (latest is NCES 2024-25).

## Column notes for school_risk_by_town.csv
`n_schools_in_town` counts operating K+ public schools with 2024-25 enrollment >0 (including schools later closing); `n_schools_remaining_after_closures` excludes those in the closure flag. `resident_adm_*` = students who live in the town (any school), FY20/FY24 AOE ADM; village-district rows (Orleans, North Bennington, Wells River) were assigned to Barton, Bennington, Newbury based on the village location; unorganized gores excluded. ESSEX TOWN / ESSEX JUNCTION CITY FY20 left blank (split in FY24).
